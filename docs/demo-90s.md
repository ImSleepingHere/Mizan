# MIZAN — 90-second live demo

One presenter, one browser window, Baseline semester. About 210 spoken words (a calm pace with pauses). The 15-second optimization runs while you speak, so it costs no extra time. Figures re-measured on a fresh database on 5 October 2026; optimizer figures can vary slightly, so say "about".

## Before you go on (5 minutes)

1. Close Mizan → `Reset Mizan Demo.cmd` → `Start Mizan.cmd`. The demo doesn't need the AI model.
2. Sign in as **Administrator**. Open **Overview** on **Fall 2026 · Baseline**, with the zoom set so the big numbers are readable from the back.
3. Open **Change requests** in a second tab, already showing **S001, Sunday 08:00 → 10:00** with a reason typed ("Professor unavailable"). Don't press Preview yet.
4. Backup: the demo video on the desktop.

## Script

| Time | On screen (do) | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:12** | Overview, nothing clicked | "A Saudi study at Prince Sultan University found that the breaks in a student's day help predict absences, and absences track lower grades. Most timetables are built to be *valid*, not to be good for students." |
| **0:12–0:27** | Point to **15,000 h**, **quality 45**, **100% with 2h+ gaps** | "This semester passes every hard rule. But students lose **15,000 hours a week** waiting between classes. That's 10 hours each. The data is synthetic, and deliberately poor." |
| **0:27–0:47** | Click **Optimize semester** → **Run optimization** (5 changes, 15 s). Talk while it runs. | "Mizan searches for better valid timetables. Every candidate is re-checked by an independent validator, and nothing reaches students without a person's approval. It all runs on this laptop, with no cloud AI." |
| **0:47–1:02** | The proposal opens. Point to **about 1,800 h recovered**, **225 better off**, **0 worse off**, **0 conflicts** | "Five changes recover about **1,800 student-hours a week**. **225 students** are better off and **nobody is worse off**. You see the benefit and the harm, side by side." |
| **1:02–1:10** | **Approve proposal** → **Revalidate & publish locally** | "The committee approves. Publishing re-checks everything first." |
| **1:10–1:23** | Switch to the second tab → **Preview impact**: **77 conflicts**, then **Feasible alternatives** | "Mid-semester, a professor asks to move one class. Mizan shows it would create **77 conflicts**, explains why, and offers three valid alternatives instead." |
| **1:23–1:30** | Press **العربية** (everything flips to Arabic, right to left) | "Fully bilingual. Measure the cost, recommend a change, show the evidence, and a person decides. That's Mizan." |

## Rules

- Say "about 1,800" and "synthetic data". Never say "optimal" on its own, and never say Mizan raises GPA: the study is a correlation.
- **Running long:** skip the Arabic switch and end on the 77-conflict screen with the closing line.
- **Running short:** at 1:02, scroll to **Proposed changes** and say "every moved class is listed, before and after".
- **If the optimizer is slow or fails:** "The search is time-limited, and the current timetable is always kept." Then go straight to the change-request tab.
