## Questions (2 min): who answers

| Topic | Owner | Short answer |
|---|---|---|
| Is 15,000 hours real? | Sultan | "No, the demo semester is synthetic and deliberately poor. The research on slide 2 is real; the demo shows the method." |
| Does it raise grades? | Sultan | "We don't claim that. The research links timetables to attendance; grades are only correlated." |
| What did fine-tuning do? | Ali | "A better prompt took it from 75 to 96 of 120; fine-tuning added about 10 (106). Synthetic benchmark; code decides the outcome." |
| Does Ask Mizan understand requests? | Ali | "It drafts an interpretation the professor checks; 7 of 30 were fully right on our hand-written test, so a person always confirms." |
| Is student data safe? | Ali | "Everything runs on this laptop. Professors never see student IDs; uploaded files aren't stored." |
| Real university data? | Mishary | "We import real Edugate schedules today; instructors and room sizes must be verified before anything is published. A pilot would connect real data." |
| Staffing example | Mishary | "36 of the 112 students who need Machine Learning would have no seat; that's a proven shortage, so recruitment can start." |
| Cost / deployment | Mishary | "One university PC, no cloud fees, no external AI." |

**Never say:** "111 vs 75", "understands", "112 at risk", "optimal" on its own, "official Edugate record".

## Before you go on stage

1. Laptop plugged in; Mizan started (`Start Mizan.cmd`); demo data reset beforehand (`Reset Mizan Demo.cmd`, with Mizan closed).
2. Logged in as **Scheduling committee** or **Administrator**, on the Overview, browser zoomed so the numbers are readable.
3. An Edugate import already done and optimized, so the Edugate PDF download is instant. Keep the app screenshot file on the desktop for the upload.
4. Run Optimize once on this laptop beforehand to confirm "about 1,800 hours" (the AI model isn't needed for the demo).
5. Backup: the demo video on the desktop. If the app fails, play it instead of the live demo.
6. Practise the switch: PowerPoint → browser after slide 4, and back to PowerPoint on slide 5 (Trust). Keep the browser in its own full-screen window.
7. One full timed rehearsal with all three of you.
