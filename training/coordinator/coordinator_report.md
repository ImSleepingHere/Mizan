# Coordinator fine-tuning experiment: findings

Frozen synthetic test set: 120 cases (balanced English/Arabic). Same official Qwen3-8B base in NF4, greedy decoding, 128-token cap, with vs. without the selected LoRA adapter.

Training: selected epoch 2 by validation loss 0.0258; 120 optimizer steps; 12.6 min; peak GPU 6.493 GiB; 7,667,712 trainable parameters.

## Reading these results

- This is a small synthetic benchmark generated by the same state generator as the training data. It measures generalization within the implemented coordinator workflow, not real-world ability.
- A gain here does not prove deployment improvement and does not justify removing the production schema checks.
- The adapter stays outside the live application until you explicitly approve it.
