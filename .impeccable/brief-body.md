## Direction contract

THESIS: The semester as a calm information system (Swiss/Aicher lineage): one grid, one Sun–Thu hour scale, flat colour fields that carry meaning. Refuses the category default of gradient SaaS cards with glows and a soft KPI grid.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground #f6f6f3, ink #111 sidebar with white inverted active item, four flat signal colours (blue #1d3cff action/primary, green, orange, red) used as fields and strips, never gradients, glass or glow. Archivo in light/regular weights (thin large numerals, small labels), Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic. Square corners, 1px hairlines, no shadows except the lifted timetable ghost and modal. Every graded number is coloured on a continuous red→amber→green OKLCH scale defined per metric (bad/good anchors), never a hard threshold.

STORY: The viewer sees the cost (student hours lost to gaps, graded), sees which cohorts suffer on the colour-coded bars, sees that proposals are waiting, opens one, reads before/after evidence with the Preview → Propose → Approve → Publish station strip, and approves. Humans decide.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview: 4-colour department strip on top edge; heading "Every hour of the week, in balance." left with live dot + semester/scenario line, blue "Optimize semester" block right. Below: 68px light numeral of weekly gap hours (graded) left; three colour-topped stat tiles (quality, 2h+ gaps, room use) right, values graded. Then cohort bars in department colours with graded values; then a single "N proposals waiting → Review" line with an orange count block.

FORM: Information System (Swiss/Aicher), position 7 of the ordered list, seed key 8a8588bc; refined by the user into "A4": A2's calm light type + A's colours + motion; user added continuous graded number colours. Raises kept: station strip (darkroom), registered before/after (botanical), one hour axis (dive), fixed section label grid (sneaker), inversion as selection (one-bit).

Signature interaction: live graded numbers that count up and settle into their grade colour; timetable drag with live preview ghost turning green/red.
